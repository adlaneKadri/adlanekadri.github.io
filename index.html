Project  ·  2020 — 2021  ·  Innoscape

Multi-label classification of consumer reviews using NLP

Master's thesis, carried out over two internships — Sorbonne Paris Nord, then Paris 8

The problem

Retail brands need to know what customers actually talk about in their product reviews — price, build quality, instructions, ease of use, delivery. At the volume these reviews arrive in, reading them is impossible, and the corpus arrives with no labels at all.

What I built
Collection at scale
crawlers across e-commerce sources, through anti-bot defences
Corpus & preprocessing
~300K reviews · 60K-word vocabulary
Automatic labelling
topic modelling (NMF, LDA) over TF-IDF and bag-of-words
Cluster-to-label mapping
feature engineering to turn topics into business categories
Supervised classifiers
Random Forest · Gradient Boosting · multilayer perceptron
Deployment
model selection, then integration into the production workflow
The result

The unsupervised stage removed the need to hand-label a corpus far too large to annotate manually. Comparing representations, TF-IDF with NMF clearly outperformed bag-of-words with LDA (93.4% against 87.9%), and the final tuned classifiers reached 96–98% accuracy across the six review categories.
